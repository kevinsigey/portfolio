Stablecoin Remittance Monitor

Five saved Dune queries track the cost and shape of stablecoin transfers on Ethereum mainnet, measured against the World Bank remittance benchmarks. This page is the running record.

Kevin Sigey · Data as of October 1, 2026 · Refreshed monthly

The monitor asks one question on a schedule: is moving money on-chain still cheaper than the systems remittances actually use? The benchmark lines are the planning figures from the paper, $7.00 for a digital competitor and $14.00 for the traditional channel. The measured line is the observed network cost of a transfer plus a modeled $1.50 off-ramp.

As of the October 1, 2026 run, the measured cost sits under both benchmarks, as it has on all but one of the 184 days on record. The usage data is narrower: about one transfer in five is remittance-sized, and those transfers carry under half a percent of the value moved. Both facts stay on this page and update together.

1 · The cost line

Daily measured cost per transfer, Apr 1 to Oct 1, 2026

Daily cost from Dune query 1: observed Ethereum mainnet network cost plus a modeled $1.50 off-ramp, against the $7.00 digital and $14.00 traditional benchmark lines and the $0.51 L2 theoretical line. Data as of October 1, 2026.

The line ranged from $1.77 to $7.12 across 184 days. It crossed the digital benchmark once, on June 5, and never reached the traditional line. It closed the window at $2.11; the thirty-day average to October 1 is $2.76.

2 · Where the activity sits

Share of $10–$1,000 transfers by UTC activity window, three months to Oct 1, 2026

Dune query 2, 28.1 million transfers between $10 and $1,000. The region labels are working-hours proxies, not user locations: a transfer inside EMEA hours is not evidence of a European sender. Data as of October 1, 2026.

No window dominates. EMEA working hours carry the largest share at 29.1%; the Americas window, the paper's US-to-LatAm corridor proxy, carries 26.8%.

4 · Status and method

The monitor runs on five saved Dune queries: cost, activity windows, labeled flows, transfer sizes and token preference. A scheduled pipeline re-runs them monthly; the SQL and the refresh script are public in the project repository.

Latest completed run: October 1, 2026. The November 1 run failed against Dune's free-tier datapoint limit, so every figure on this page stands as of October 1, 2026 until the next run lands. Nothing here is estimated between runs.

The full study behind the monitor, including the labeling limits and the skeptical CFO review, is the paper: The Digital Remittance Bridge.

Sources and method. Dune Analytics queries 8871549, 8871565, 8871571, 8871577 and 8871590 (@kevinsigey): Ethereum mainnet ERC-20 transfer events joined to transaction gas and daily ETH prices. Benchmarks are the World Bank Remittance Prices Worldwide planning lines as used in the paper; the L2 figure is a model, not an observation. Figures are labeled as of the October 1, 2026 run.
